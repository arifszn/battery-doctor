Connect your phone for a battery checkup

One setting on the phone lets your computer read its battery records over the cable. It takes about two minutes, and you can turn it off again afterwards.

No "Allow USB debugging?" prompt

Try these in order.

  1. Check that USB debugging is still on (step 3), and that the phone is unlocked.
  2. Unplug the cable and plug it in again.
  3. Pull down the notification shade, tap the USB notification and choose File transfer.
  4. Use a different cable. A charge-only cable will never show the prompt.
  5. Use a different USB port, directly on the computer and not through a hub.
  6. In Developer options, tap Revoke USB debugging authorizations, then plug in again.
  7. On Windows, install the phone maker's USB driver. Your assistant has the link.

When you are finished with the checkup you can turn USB debugging off again in Developer options.
